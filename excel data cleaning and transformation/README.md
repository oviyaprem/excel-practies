The following data-cleaning operations were performed: 

Handling Missing Values: Checked the Price and Category columns for missing information and identified values requiring further investigation. 
Correcting Inconsistent Data: Standardized product names and category formats and corrected spelling inconsistencies. 
Removing Duplicates: Identified and removed duplicate records to maintain data accuracy. 
Splitting and Merging Data: Separated Product ID into Manufacturing Date and Country Code. Combined Brand Name and Product Name into a single column called Product Brand. 
Number Formatting: Formatted Price as currency and Manufacturing Date in DD-MM-YYYY format. 
Conditional Formatting: Applied data bars or color scales to the Price column and highlighted products belonging to the Electronics category.
